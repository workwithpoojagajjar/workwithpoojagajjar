## Why This Book Caught My Attention

Throughout my professional journey in restaurant operations and corporate training, I've become increasingly interested in understanding what makes businesses successful.

One question particularly interests me:

**How do successful organizations develop people who consistently deliver exceptional experiences?**

This is what drew my attention to Disney University.

## Training Is More Than Teaching Procedures

Training is often associated with teaching employees how to complete specific tasks.

But I believe effective employee development should go beyond procedures.

It should help employees understand the purpose behind their responsibilities, the organization's expectations, and how their individual contributions influence the customer experience.

## The Connection Between People and Business Performance

Operational excellence requires more than documented standards.

It depends on employees understanding those standards and applying them consistently.

In my experience, communication, coaching, and practical training play important roles in supporting that consistency.

## My Business Perspective

I believe organizations should evaluate employee development not only as a training activity but also as part of their broader business strategy.

When businesses invest in building capable teams, they create opportunities to strengthen operational execution, customer experiences, and long-term performance.

## Final Thoughts

For me, exploring Disney University's approach is an opportunity to think more deeply about the relationship between people, organizational culture, and sustainable business growth.

**Because strong businesses aren't built through strategies alone. They're built through people who bring those strategies to life.**
