Why Business Growth Starts with Operational Excellence

A perspective by Pooja Gajjar · October 2026

When businesses talk about growth, the conversation often begins with expansion, revenue, or market share. Those outcomes matter. But growth becomes more sustainable when the daily operation can deliver on the promise being made to customers.

Consistency is a growth capability

A business can have an excellent concept and still disappoint customers when execution changes from one location or shift to another. Clear standards, capable teams, and practical feedback loops make consistency possible without eliminating local judgment.

Look at the frontline

Frontline observations reveal friction that spreadsheets may not capture: unclear handoffs, incomplete onboarding, or procedures that are difficult to follow during busy periods. Listening to employees and customers helps leaders decide where improvement is most useful.

Connect observation to action

I believe operational excellence is a cycle: observe, identify the cause, test a practical change, coach the team, and revisit the result. A recommendation only becomes valuable when people can act on it.

My experience in restaurant operations shaped this perspective. Sustainable growth depends not only on the strategy at the top, but also on the confidence and consistency of the people delivering it every day.
